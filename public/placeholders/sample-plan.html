01 - The Problem

Appointment processing at DepEd SDO Cavite was a manual, paper-and-spreadsheet operation. Personnel data was tracked across multiple Excel sheets with no validation, official documents were filled out by hand from CSC templates, and transaction numbers were recorded without automated duplicate detection. There was no central source of truth, role-based access control, audit trail, or way to enforce the required appointment lifecycle, leading to rework, lost records, and compliance risk.

02 - The Plan

Before writing code, the architecture was mapped to a lightweight Laravel MVC design that mirrors the existing DepEd document and approval workflow while preserving the role distinctions between Administrator and HR Officer.

  1. Define the data model and lifecycle

    Model a single appointments table for CSC PSIPOP fields and a record_state enum covering new, active, in progress, completed, concluded, and deleted records.

  2. Lock down access with RBAC

    Implement role middleware and an appointment policy so administrators can manage records while HR Officers can encode, edit, conclude, and generate documents. Replace public registration with an email-invitation flow.

  3. Automate Plantilla and salary lookups

    Import the master Excel data into a plantilla_items table and wire autocomplete endpoints for positions, schools, salary grades, steps, and monthly rates.

  4. Generate documents from official templates

    Use PHPWord for DOCX files and PhpSpreadsheet for XLSX files, with template selection based on SHS, secondary, and non-teaching flags.

  5. Wire up workflow and exports

    Track document downloads, prevent duplicate transaction numbers, and add bulk CSV, ZIP, and monthly monitoring exports.

  6. Build the dashboard and history

    Provide KPI cards for encoded, completed, and monthly records, plus a searchable history view for concluded and archived appointments.

> Key decision: inject data into the official DepEd and CSC template files using their existing placeholder syntax instead of re-implementing the form layouts. This preserves the required output format.

03 - What Was Built

Appointment lifecycle engine

Single-source appointments table with a state machine, soft deletes, and audit timestamps for document downloads.

Role-based access control

Administrator and HR Officer roles enforced by middleware and policy, with invitation-token registration.

Plantilla and salary automation

Excel import, live autocomplete, and salary-grade and step lookup with DepEd position mappings.

Automated document generation

PHPWord and PhpSpreadsheet engines that produce appointment forms, checklists, RAI, and deliberation documents.

Bulk export and ZIP bundler

CSV streaming, multi-appointment ZIP archives, and consolidated monthly reports.

KPI dashboard and history

DashboardService metrics, a six-month trend chart, and filtered history for concluded records.
